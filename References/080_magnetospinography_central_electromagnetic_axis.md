# Reference 080 — Magnetospinography and the Electromagnetic Activity of the Spinal Axis

## Source

Sumiya S, Kawabata S, Hoshino Y, et al.

**Magnetospinography visualizes electrophysiological activity in the cervical spinal cord.**

Scientific Reports. 2017;7:2192.

DOI: 10.1038/s41598-017-02406-8

https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-017-02406-8

Open-access full text:

https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5438392/

## Relevance to the text

The researchers used a 120-channel superconducting quantum interference device (SQUID) biomagnetometer to measure extremely weak magnetic fields generated by electrophysiological activity in the human spinal cord.

From the measured spinal cord evoked magnetic fields, propagating electrical action currents were reconstructed and spatially mapped.

The experiment demonstrates that electrical activity propagating along the human spinal axis produces measurable external biomagnetic fields.

Magnetospinography therefore provides direct experimental evidence that the central spinal axis of the human body is associated with measurable electrophysiological and biomagnetic activity.

The study does not establish the existence of a permanent independent "energy column"; it measures magnetic fields associated with propagating neural electrical currents.
