**Self-potential signals associated with pumping tests experiments.**

## Relevance

The study demonstrates experimentally that groundwater flow during a pumping test produces a measurable electrical field at the ground surface through electrokinetic coupling between groundwater flow and electrical current density.

The researchers measured the surface electrical response using a network of 53 non-polarizable electrodes.

The resulting self-potential signals were used to track subsurface groundwater-flow patterns and preferential fluid-flow pathways.

This reference supports the statement in *Merljiva nauka* that movement of groundwater through porous rock can generate an electrical signal measurable at the surface.
