# Reference 003 — Quantum Vacuum and Vacuum Fluctuations

## Source

American Physical Society (APS) — Physics

## Relevance

In quantum physics, a vacuum is not equivalent to absolute nothingness.

The quantum vacuum is associated with the lowest-energy state of quantum fields, and quantum fields can exhibit vacuum (zero-point) fluctuations even in this state.

Experimental phenomena such as the Lamb shift provide physical evidence of effects associated with vacuum fluctuations.

Therefore, the physical concept of a vacuum should not be confused with the philosophical concept of absolute nothingness.
