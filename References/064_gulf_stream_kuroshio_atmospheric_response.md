# Reference 064 — Atmospheric response above the Gulf Stream and Kuroshio

Parfitt, R., & Seo, H. (2018).

**A New Framework for Near-Surface Wind Convergence Over the Kuroshio Extension and Gulf Stream in Wintertime: The Role of Atmospheric Fronts.**

Geophysical Research Letters, 45, 9909–9918.

DOI: 10.1029/2018GL080135

## Relevance

The study examines the atmospheric response above two major western boundary current systems: the Gulf Stream and the Kuroshio Extension.

Observations show pronounced near-surface wind convergence associated with the strong sea-surface-temperature fronts of both current systems.

The atmospheric imprint also appears in precipitation and tropospheric vertical velocity fields, while ocean-to-atmosphere forcing is associated with turbulent heat fluxes across the ocean surface.

This reference supports the statement in *Merljiva nauka* that the paths of major ocean currents and their temperature fronts leave a measurable signature in the atmosphere above them.
