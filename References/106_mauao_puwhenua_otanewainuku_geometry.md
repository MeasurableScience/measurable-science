# Mauao–Pūwhenua–Ōtanewainuku Geometry

## Purpose

This reference documents the coordinates and mathematical calculations used to examine the geometric relationship between Pūwhenua, Ōtanewainuku and Mauao.

The numerical calculation is kept separate from the interpretation presented in the book so that the geometry can be independently reproduced and checked.

---

## 1. Coordinates

Coordinates are given in WGS 84.

| Point | Latitude | Longitude |
|---|---:|---:|
| Pūwhenua summit | −37.92693° | 176.11761° |
| Ōtanewainuku summit | −37.90350° | 176.20417° |
| Mauao summit | −37.63030° | 176.17198° |
| Northernmost point of Mauao | −37.6247667° | 176.1724500° |

For the radial comparison, the northernmost point of Mauao is used rather than the highest point of the mountain.

This distinction is important because the summit and the northern geographical extent of Mauao are not at the same location.

---

## 2. Pūwhenua–Ōtanewainuku Baseline

Using the WGS 84 coordinates above, the distance between Pūwhenua and Ōtanewainuku is approximately:

r₀ = 8.023 km

This distance is used as the initial radius for the golden-ratio test.

The golden ratio is:

φ = (1 + √5) / 2

φ = 1.6180339887...

Therefore:

φ² = 2.6180339887...

φ³ = 4.2360679775...

---

## 3. Golden-Ratio Rings

Starting with:

r₀ = 8.023 km

the successive golden-ratio radii are:

r₁ = φ × r₀ ≈ 12.98 km

r₂ = φ² × r₀ ≈ 21.00 km

r₃ = φ³ × r₀ ≈ 33.986 km

The third golden-ratio radius therefore predicts a position approximately 33.986 km from Pūwhenua.

---

## 4. Comparison with Mauao

The calculated distance from Pūwhenua to the northernmost point of Mauao is approximately:

rM = 33.943 km

The difference between the third golden-ratio radius and the northernmost point of Mauao is:

Δr = 33.986 − 33.943

Δr ≈ 0.043 km

Δr ≈ 43 m

The relative difference is:

(0.043 / 33.986) × 100 ≈ 0.13%

Therefore:

Pūwhenua → Mauao ≈ φ³ × (Pūwhenua → Ōtanewainuku)

The agreement is within approximately 0.13%.

---

## 5. Mauao Summit and Northernmost Point

The highest point of Mauao is not its northernmost geographical point.

The summit is located approximately at:

37.63030° S, 176.17198° E

The northernmost point of Mauao is approximately:

37.6247667° S, 176.1724500° E

The distance from Pūwhenua to the summit of Mauao is approximately 33.3 km.

The φ³ comparison therefore does not coincide with the highest point of Mauao.

Instead, the φ³ radius reaches the northern end of the mountain, where the difference between the calculated φ³ radius and the geographical position of Mauao is approximately 43 m.

---

## 6. Polar Reference

A second geometric relationship appears when the polar reference used in the model is introduced.

The reference point designated in the model as Polus Magnetis is:

75° N, 179° W

The northern orientation from Mauao is directed toward this polar reference region.

When the directions associated with Pūwhenua–Mauao and Ōtanewainuku are extended toward the polar region on the azimuthal construction used in the model, they do not form exactly the same line.

The Pūwhenua–Mauao direction passes slightly to one side of the polar reference, while the direction associated with Ōtanewainuku passes slightly to the other.

The two directions therefore form a narrow scissors-like geometry around the polar reference point.

This directional relationship is independent of the φ³ radial calculation above and is treated as a separate geometric observation.

---

## 7. Summary of the Geometric Result

The measured baseline is:

Pūwhenua → Ōtanewainuku ≈ 8.023 km

The third golden-ratio radius is:

8.023 × φ³ ≈ 33.986 km

The measured distance to the northernmost point of Mauao is:

Pūwhenua → Mauao ≈ 33.943 km

Difference:

≈ 43 m

Relative difference:

≈ 0.13%

The resulting relationship can therefore be written simply as:

Pūwhenua → Mauao ≈ φ³ × (Pūwhenua → Ōtanewainuku)

A second, independent geometric observation concerns the orientation of the same group of locations toward the polar reference at 75° N, 179° W.

The numerical geometry documented here does not by itself establish the physical cause or intentionality of these relationships. Their possible function is considered separately in the model presented in the book.
