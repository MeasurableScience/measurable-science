# Reference 009 — Atom

## Purpose

Reference for the standard scientific description of the atom used in
*Merljiva Nauka*.

## Atomic structure

An atom is the smallest unit of a chemical element that retains the
chemical properties of that element.

Atoms consist of a positively charged nucleus surrounded by electrons.

The nucleus contains protons and, in most atoms, neutrons.

The number of protons in the nucleus is the atomic number and determines
which chemical element the atom represents.

## Hydrogen

The simplest atom is hydrogen-1 (protium).

Its nucleus consists of a single proton and contains no neutron.

A neutral hydrogen-1 atom consists of:

1 proton + 1 electron

This makes hydrogen-1 the simplest example of an atom formed from a
positively charged nucleus and a negatively charged electron.

## Electric interaction

The proton carries positive electric charge.

The electron carries negative electric charge.

The electromagnetic interaction binds electrons to atomic nuclei and
is fundamental to atomic structure.
