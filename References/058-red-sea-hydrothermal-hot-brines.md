# Reference 058 — Red Sea Hydrothermal Hot Brines

Brewer, P. G. (1971).
Red Sea Brines.

Hydrographic observations of the Atlantis II Deep documented extremely hot, saline deep brines in the Red Sea.

MEASUREMENTS:

At depths greater than approximately 2000 m, the Atlantis II Deep contains hot brine layers.

Measured deep-brine temperature increased from approximately:

56.5 °C

to:

59.2 °C

during a 51-month observation interval.

The observations demonstrate the natural coexistence of:

- deep water
- very high salinity
- elevated temperature
- hydrothermal/geological activity

USED IN THE BOOK:

The Red Sea provides a directly measured natural example of extremely hot and saline deep water associated with an active geological and hydrothermal environment.
