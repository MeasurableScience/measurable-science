# Reference 020 — Deep melting at approximately 250 km

## Source

Nature (2025).

High-pressure experimental study of deep silicate melting.

DOI / source:
https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-10065-3

## Relevant finding

High-pressure experiments indicate that silicate melting can begin
under conditions corresponding to depths of approximately 250 km,
at pressures of roughly 7 GPa.

The experiments demonstrate that partially molten silicate material
can exist under pressures corresponding to hundreds of kilometres
of depth.

## Relevance to the book

This reference supports the statement that molten or partially molten
material is physically possible at depths on the order of 250 km.

It does not establish the geometry, origin, or scale of the deep
subsurface system proposed by the author.
