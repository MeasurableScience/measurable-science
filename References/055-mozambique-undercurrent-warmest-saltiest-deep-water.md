# Reference 055 — Mozambique Undercurrent: Warmest and Most Saline Deep Water

Ullgren, J. E., van Aken, H. M., Ridderinkhof, H., & de Ruijter, W. P. M. (2012).

The hydrography of the Mozambique Channel from six years of continuous temperature, salinity, and velocity observations.

Deep-Sea Research Part I: Oceanographic Research Papers,
69, 36–50.

DOI:
10.1016/j.dsr.2012.07.003

MEASUREMENTS:

More than six years of continuous observations of:

- temperature
- salinity
- current velocity

were analysed across the Mozambique Channel.

KEY RESULT:

The warmest and most saline deep water was found within the northward-flowing Mozambique Undercurrent on the western side of the channel.

The undercurrent contains two cores:

- an intermediate core
- a deep core

The deep core contains North Atlantic Deep Water.

USED IN THE BOOK:

The northward-flowing Mozambique Undercurrent contains the warmest and most saline deep water measured across the section.
