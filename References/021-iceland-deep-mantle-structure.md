# Reference 021 — Deep structure beneath Iceland

## Source

U.S. Geological Survey.

Imaging the mantle beneath Iceland using integrated seismological
techniques.

## Relevant finding

Seismological observations identify an anomalous structure beneath
Iceland extending through approximately the upper 250 km.

A narrower continuation of the anomaly can be traced to depths
approaching approximately 400 km.

## Relevance to the book

This reference demonstrates that structures associated with volcanic
and thermal systems can be investigated seismologically through
hundreds of kilometres of depth.

The observation does not establish the author's proposed subsurface
geometry or imply that the observed Icelandic structure is identical
to the system developed in the book.
