# Reference 002 — Einstein and Mass–Energy Equivalence

## Source

Albert Einstein (1905)

**"Ist die Trägheit eines Körpers von seinem Energieinhalt abhängig?"**  
*Annalen der Physik*, 18, 639–641.

## Relevance

In this 1905 paper, Einstein established the relationship between
the energy content of a body and its mass.

This work is the historical basis of the mass–energy equivalence
commonly expressed as:

**E = mc²**

The relation describes the equivalence between mass and energy.
It does not, by itself, provide an explanation for the origin of mass.
