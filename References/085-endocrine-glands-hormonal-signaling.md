## Relevance to the text

Endocrine glands produce chemical signaling molecules known as hormones and release them directly into the bloodstream.

These hormones travel through the circulation to distant tissues and organs, where they bind to specific receptors and produce physiological responses.

The endocrine system therefore provides a biological mechanism through which information generated in one part of the organism can produce physical changes in distant parts of the body.

This supports the description in the text of endocrine glands as biological output points through which regulatory information is translated into changes in metabolism, growth, reproduction and other bodily functions.
