# Reference 056 — Somali Basin Deep Circulation

Beal, L. M., Molinari, R. L., Chereskin, T. K., & Robbins, P. E. (2000).

Reversing bottom circulation in the Somali Basin.

Geophysical Research Letters,
27(16), 2565–2568.

MEASUREMENTS:

Direct deep-current measurements were made in the northern Somali Basin.

The observations demonstrate energetic deep circulation and show that the direction of the bottom boundary current can vary.

During one occupation a southward deep western boundary current of approximately 5 Sv was measured.

During another occupation the boundary flow reversed and approximately:

2.6 Sv

was measured northward.

USED IN THE BOOK:

Deep water circulation in the Somali Basin is directly measured and can include substantial northward transport.

The direction is variable and should not be interpreted as a permanently northward current.
