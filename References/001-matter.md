# Reference 001 — Matter, particles and fundamental interactions

## Source

CERN — The Standard Model

## Relevance

CERN describes matter as being composed of elementary particles,
grouped into quarks and leptons, and identifies four fundamental
forces in nature: the strong, weak, electromagnetic and gravitational
interactions.

The Standard Model itself describes three of these interactions;
gravity is not included in the Standard Model.
