# Reference 017 — Radiogenic heat from uranium, thorium and potassium

## Source

Duffield, W. A., Sass, J. H., & Sorey, M. L. (1994).
*Tapping the Earth's Natural Heat.*
U.S. Geological Survey Circular 1125.

DOI: 10.3133/cir1125

## Relevant statement

The U.S. Geological Survey states that heat is continuously produced at
depth primarily by the decay of naturally radioactive elements,
principally uranium, thorium and potassium, which occur in rocks.

## Relevance to the book

This supports the scientific statement that naturally occurring
uranium, thorium and potassium provide a persistent internal source
of heat through radioactive decay.

It does not establish the author's proposed geometry or arrangement
of the primordial material plane; that part belongs to the model
developed in the book.
