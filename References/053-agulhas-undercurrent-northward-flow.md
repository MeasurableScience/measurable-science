# Reference 053 — Agulhas Undercurrent Northward Flow

Beal, L. M. (2009).
A Time Series of Agulhas Undercurrent Transport.
Journal of Physical Oceanography, 39, 2436–2450.

MEASUREMENTS:

A 550-day record was constructed from five deep moorings on the continental slope off South Africa.

The Agulhas Undercurrent flows northeastward/equatorward beneath the southwestward Agulhas Current.

Average transport:

4.2 ± 5.2 Sv

Peak measured speeds:

> 0.90 m/s at approximately 1400 m depth.

Estimated dimensions:

approximately 40 km wide
approximately 2000 m vertical scale

The study states that the variability of the undercurrent is strongly controlled by bottom topography.

USED IN THE BOOK:

Agulhas surface flow → south.

Agulhas Undercurrent → north/equatorward.

Average undercurrent transport ≈ 4.2 Sv.

Peak deep velocities > 0.90 m/s.
