## Relevant measurement

The study used hydrographic measurements from the four major
gateways surrounding the Arctic Ocean:

- Bering Strait
- Davis Strait
- Fram Strait
- Barents Sea Opening

The measurements were obtained within the same 32-day period
during summer 2005 and were used to construct a quasi-synoptic
volume budget for the Arctic Ocean.

The authors report:

"The volume transport into the Arctic of waters above ~1000 m
depth is 9.2 Sv (1 Sv = 10^6 m3 s-1)."

The corresponding export was estimated at 9.3 Sv.


## Working value used in the book

For the simplified physical model developed in the book,
the measured inflow of 9.2 Sv is rounded conservatively to:

9 Sv

where:

1 Sv = 1,000,000 m3/s

Therefore:

9 Sv = 9,000,000 m3/s.


## Relevance to the book

This reference supports the order of magnitude used for the
quantity of ocean water entering the Arctic system.

The measurement does not establish that this water enters the
hypothetical central drainage structure proposed in the book.

The use of 9 Sv as the flow through that proposed structure is
an assumption of the author's model and is tested separately
through subsequent calculations.
