## Relevance to the text

Development of the human adrenal system begins very early in embryogenesis.

The adrenal cortex and adrenal medulla have different embryological origins.

The cortex develops from mesoderm associated with the urogenital/adrenogonadal region.

Cells contributing to the future adrenal medulla derive from neural crest lineages and migrate into the developing adrenal gland.

The mature adrenal gland therefore combines tissues derived through distinct developmental pathways into a single functional organ.

The adrenal cortex produces steroid hormones, while the medulla produces catecholamines such as epinephrine and norepinephrine, allowing the adrenal system to participate in metabolism, stress responses and rapid physiological adaptation.
