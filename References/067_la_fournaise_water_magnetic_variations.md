# Reference 067 — Water circulation and magnetic variations at La Fournaise

Zlotnicki, J., & Le Mouël, J. L. (1990).

**Possible electrokinetic origin of large magnetic variations at La Fournaise volcano.**

Nature, 343, 633–636.

DOI: 10.1038/343633a0

## Relevance

The study analyses more than three years of geomagnetic observations from Piton de la Fournaise volcano on Réunion Island.

The authors considered both piezomagnetic and electrokinetic mechanisms for the observed volcanomagnetic variations.

Their analysis led them to identify water circulation as the main candidate responsible for the strong magnetic signals observed at the volcano.

They proposed that the magnetic variations were generated through electrokinetic effects, with the water-circulation system inside the volcanic massif responding to stress changes associated with volcanic activity.

This reference supports the statement in *Merljiva nauka* that circulation of water within an active volcanic system can be associated with measurable magnetic-field variations.
