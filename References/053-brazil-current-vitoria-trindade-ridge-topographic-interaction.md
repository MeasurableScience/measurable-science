# Reference 053 — Brazil Current Interaction with the Vitória–Trindade Ridge

## Purpose

This reference supports the statements in the book concerning direct observations of the Brazil Current interacting with the Vitória–Trindade Ridge.

Oceanographic measurements across the Vitória–Trindade Ridge include observations of:

- horizontal velocity,
- density,
- temperature,
- salinity,
- vertical structure of the current.

The observations show that the Brazil Current interacts strongly with the complex seamount topography of the ridge.

The interaction between the current and the submarine topography produces deeper dynamical disturbances and vertical transport.

This demonstrates that the influence of the ridge is not restricted to a simple surface deflection of the current.

The current-topography interaction can affect the vertical structure of the surrounding water column.

## Relevance to the model

The book searches for measurable interaction between major warm ocean currents and large submarine geological structures.

At the Vitória–Trindade Ridge, the Brazil Current encounters a major submarine ridge/seamount system and its interaction with that topography produces measurable dynamical effects extending beneath the surface current.

This observation is used only as evidence of current-topography interaction.

It is not evidence that the volcanic structure supplies the heat of the Brazil Current.
