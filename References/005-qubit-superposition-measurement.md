## Relevance

A classical bit has one of two values: 0 or 1.

A qubit can instead be prepared in a quantum superposition of the basis states |0⟩ and |1⟩.

When the qubit is measured in the computational basis, the measurement produces one of two classical outcomes:

**0 or 1**

The probabilities of these outcomes depend on the amplitudes of the qubit's quantum state.

Measurement therefore connects a quantum state containing multiple possible measurement outcomes with a definite observed result.

In *Merljiva nauka*, this physical process is used as an analogy for the distinction between potential possibilities and an observed outcome. The interpretation of this process as "potential" is part of the model presented in the book, not a standard definition in quantum mechanics.
