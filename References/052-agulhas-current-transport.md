# Reference 052 — Agulhas Current Transport

Bryden, H. L., Beal, L. M., & Duncan, L. M. (2005).
Structure and Transport of the Agulhas Current and Its Temporal Variability.
Journal of Oceanography, 61, 479–492.

DOI:
10.1007/s10872-005-0057-8

MEASURED RESULT:

Long-term moored observations across the Agulhas Current gave an average total transport of approximately:

69.7 Sv

from the surface to 2400 m depth.

The study describes the Agulhas Current as the largest western boundary current in the world ocean based on comparable time-series measurements near 30° latitude.
