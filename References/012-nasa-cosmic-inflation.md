# [12] NASA — Cosmic Inflation and the Unknown State Before Inflation

## Source

NASA Science. “Early Universe — Cosmic Inflation.”

NASA states that observations of the early universe support a period called cosmic inflation, during which the universe underwent extremely rapid expansion.

NASA also explicitly states that scientists are not sure what came before inflation or what powered it.

According to NASA, when cosmic inflation ended, the energy driving inflation was transferred to matter and light, marking the beginning of the phase commonly associated with the Big Bang.

## Relevance to the book

This source is used to distinguish between what modern cosmology models about the early development of the universe and what remains unknown.

In particular, it supports the statement that contemporary cosmology does not currently have a confirmed explanation for:

- what preceded cosmic inflation;
- what powered inflation;
- the physical origin of the initial state from which the observable universe developed.

The book uses this acknowledged boundary of current cosmological knowledge as the point from which alternative interpretations of the primordial state are examined.
