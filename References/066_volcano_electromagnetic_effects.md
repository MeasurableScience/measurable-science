## Relevance

This review describes electromagnetic signals generated by volcanic activity and identifies multiple physical mechanisms capable of producing them.

These include:

- piezomagnetic effects
- electrokinetic effects
- fluid vaporization
- thermal demagnetization and remagnetization
- electrical resistivity changes
- thermochemical effects
- magnetohydrodynamic effects

The review also explains that electromagnetic monitoring is used together with seismic, deformation, gas and other geophysical measurements to investigate volcanic processes.

This reference supports the statement in *Merljiva nauka* that active volcanic systems can produce measurable electrical and magnetic changes through several known physical mechanisms.
