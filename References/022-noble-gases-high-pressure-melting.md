# Reference 022 — Noble gases under extreme pressure

## Source

High-pressure experimental measurements of the melting behaviour
of solid argon, krypton and xenon.

Nature.

Source:
https://www.nature.com/articles/30712

## Relevant finding

Diamond-anvil-cell experiments measured the melting behaviour of
argon, krypton and xenon at pressures reaching tens of gigapascals.

The experiments demonstrate that substances normally encountered as
gases at surface conditions can exist as condensed solid phases under
sufficient pressure.

Their melting temperatures increase substantially as pressure rises.

## Relevance to the book

This reference supports treating noble gases under extreme pressure
according to their high-pressure phase behaviour rather than assuming
that they must remain gases simply because they are gaseous under
ordinary surface conditions.

It does not demonstrate that noble gases form a structural floor or
barrier capable of supporting the author's proposed material plane.
That interpretation belongs to the author's model.
