# Reference 059 — Leeuwin Undercurrent: Northward Saline Water

Woo, M., & Pattiaratchi, C. (2008).

Hydrography and water masses off the western Australian coast.

Deep-Sea Research Part I: Oceanographic Research Papers,
55(9), 1090–1104.

DOI:
10.1016/j.dsr.2008.05.005

MEASUREMENTS:

CTD and ADCP observations were used to examine the circulation and water masses along the western Australian coast.

Beneath the southward-flowing Leeuwin Current, the Leeuwin Undercurrent flows northward.

The undercurrent occurs primarily at depths of approximately:

200–400 m

and transports approximately:

5 Sv

of higher-salinity water.

Measured salinity:

> 35.8

Reported northward velocities:

0.32–0.40 m/s

Current-meter observations independently confirm the northward undercurrent beneath the Leeuwin Current.

USED IN THE BOOK:

Leeuwin Current → southward.

Leeuwin Undercurrent → northward.

Depth ≈ 200–400 m.

Transport ≈ 5 Sv.

Salinity > 35.8.
