# Reference 062 — Deep Hydrothermal Groundwater in Da Qaidam Salt Lake

Deep hydrothermal and shallow groundwater borne lithium and boron loadings to a mega brine lake in Qinghai Tibet Plateau based on multi-tracer models.

Journal of Hydrology (2021).

DOI:
10.1016/j.jhydrol.2021.126444

MEASUREMENTS:

The study investigated Da Qaidam Lake in the Qaidam Basin using:

- radium isotopes
- lithium
- boron
- hydrogeochemical tracers

Da Qaidam is a major brine lake enriched in lithium and boron.

The measurements identify groundwater discharge as an important source of these elements.

Deep groundwater occurs mainly as hydrothermal groundwater.

Lithium- and boron-rich hot spring water flows into the lake basin.

The study therefore provides direct geochemical evidence that a continental salt lake can receive dissolved material through deep hydrothermal groundwater.

USED IN THE BOOK:

Deep hydrothermal groundwater measurably contributes dissolved material to a large continental salt lake in the Qaidam Basin.
