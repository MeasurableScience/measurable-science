# Reference 023 — Noble gases in silicate melts at high pressure

## Source

Nature.

Experimental investigation of noble-gas behaviour in silicate melts
under high-pressure and high-temperature conditions.

Source:
https://www.nature.com/articles/nature04583

## Experimental conditions

Experiments investigated argon in silicate material under pressures
up to approximately 25 GPa and temperatures reaching approximately
3000 K.

## Relevant finding

The experiments demonstrate that noble gases cannot simply be treated
as freely escaping gases under deep high-pressure conditions.

Argon can interact with and be incorporated into silicate melts under
extreme pressure and temperature.

Other experimental work also demonstrates pressure-dependent noble-gas
solubility and incorporation in deep silicate materials.

## Relevance to the book

This provides an experimental basis for considering noble gases as
components of a deep, high-pressure material system.

It does not establish that noble gases form the physical base of the
author's proposed system or prevent molten material from moving
downward. Those are hypotheses developed in the book.
