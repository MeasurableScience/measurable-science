## Relevance to the text

The lymphatic system does not possess a single central pump equivalent to the heart.

Collecting lymphatic vessels are actively contractile and exhibit spontaneous rhythmic contractions.

Human mesenteric lymphatic vessels exhibit measurable electrical activity. Their lymphatic smooth muscle cells have a resting membrane potential of approximately -45 mV and generate spontaneous action potentials.

The action potentials precede phasic contractions, demonstrating direct coupling between electrical oscillation and mechanical lymphatic pumping.

Lymph propulsion is additionally influenced by pressure, surrounding tissue movement, skeletal muscle activity, respiration, and pulsation of adjacent blood vessels.

These observations support the description of the lymphatic circulation as a dynamic, electrically active and rhythmically oscillating transport system rather than a passive network of tubes.
