## 2. What established physics tells us

According to CERN's 2025 publication, the masses of the three valence quarks account for less than 1% of the total mass of protons and neutrons.

The overwhelming majority of their mass is associated with the energy of the internal quark–gluon system.

Gluons have no rest mass, yet the energy associated with gluon fields and quantum chromodynamics (QCD) contributes to the mass of the complete particle.

This relationship is consistent with Einstein's mass–energy equivalence:

**E = mc²**

The neutron's measured rest-mass energy is approximately:

**E_n = 939.565 MeV**

The essential conclusion is:

**THE MASS OF A NEUTRON IS NOT SIMPLY THE SUM OF THE MASSES OF ITS CONSTITUENT VALENCE QUARKS.**

Most of its mass is associated with the energy of the interacting system.

This includes the dynamics of quarks, gluons, and the strong interaction that governs their organization.
