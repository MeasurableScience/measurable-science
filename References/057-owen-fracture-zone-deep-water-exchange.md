# Reference 057 — Deep Water Exchange Through the Owen Fracture Zone

Quadfasel, D., Fischer, J., Schott, F., & Stramma, L. (1997).

Deep water exchange through the Owen Fracture Zone in the Arabian Sea.

Geophysical Research Letters,
24, 2805–2808.

MEASURED RESULT:

Deep-water exchange occurs between the Somali Basin and Arabian Basin through the Owen Fracture Zone.

The Owen Fracture Zone therefore provides a deep connection across the ridge system separating the two basins.

USED IN THE BOOK:

Deep water from the Somali Basin can cross through the Owen Fracture Zone into the Arabian Basin.

This allows the measured deep-water pathway to be followed into the Arabian Sea.
