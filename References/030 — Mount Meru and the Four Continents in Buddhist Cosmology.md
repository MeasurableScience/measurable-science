Reference 030 — Mount Meru and the Four Continents in Buddhist Cosmology

Source tradition

Traditional Buddhist cosmology, especially the cosmological system preserved in the Abhidharma tradition and in Tibetan Buddhist literature.

Central mountain

In this cosmological model, Mount Meru (Sumeru) stands at the centre of the world-system.

Tibetan Buddhist reference material describes Mount Meru as lying at the centre of the world, surrounded by rings of mountains and waters.

Four continents

Outside the mountain rings, in the great ocean, are four island-continents positioned around Mount Meru in the four cardinal directions.

They are:

Pūrvavideha — East

Jambudvīpa — South

Aparagodānīya — Wes
