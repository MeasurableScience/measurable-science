# Reference 063 — Hydrothermal contribution to Qarhan Salt Lake

Fan, Q. et al. (2018).  
**Sr isotope and major ion compositional evidence for formation of Qarhan Salt Lake, western China.**  
*Chemical Geology*, 497, 128–145.

DOI: 10.1016/j.chemgeo.2018.09.001

## Relevance

Geochemical and isotopic measurements from the Qarhan Salt Lake system in the Qaidam Basin provide evidence for a contribution of diagenetic-hydrothermal waters to the lake system.

The study analyses river waters, spring waters, brines and salts and identifies Ca–Cl waters associated with a diagenetic-hydrothermal origin.

This reference supports the statement in *Merljiva nauka* that some large closed saline basins of the Qaidam region contain measurable geochemical evidence of contribution from deep hydrothermal fluids.
