# Reference 054 — Mozambique Undercurrent Northward Deep Flow

Scientific observations in the Mozambique Channel identify a northward-flowing undercurrent along the western side of the channel.

The Mozambique Undercurrent contains intermediate and deep cores and forms part of the deep circulation beneath the predominantly southward-moving upper-ocean system.

Direct current measurements show northward flow at depths extending well below 1500 m.

USED IN THE BOOK:

A northward-flowing deep current exists beneath the Mozambique Channel circulation.

This provides the continuation of the deep northward-flowing system investigated beneath the Agulhas Current.
