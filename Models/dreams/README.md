# Dreams

The **Dream** model investigates the proposed interaction between the planetary Carrier and the human body during sleep.

According to the proposed model, communication between the Carrier and the human organism continues throughout the entire sleep cycle.

Rather than relying only on subjective descriptions of dreams, this project investigates whether measurable changes in the Carrier correlate with different stages of sleep.

---

# Working Hypothesis

According to the proposed model, communication between the Carrier and the human body follows a structured physiological pathway involving the body's major endocrine glands.

The complete theoretical model and the proposed biological mechanism are described in detail in the accompanying book.

The objective of this project is not to assume that the model is correct, but to investigate whether repeatable relationships can be identified between Carrier measurements and different stages of sleep.

---

# Measurements

Unlike conventional EEG measurements that record electrical activity directly from the scalp, this project investigates the Carrier itself.

Measurements are performed near the sleeping subject throughout the night while documenting sleep behaviour and significant physiological or subjective events.

The objective is to determine whether repeatable correlations exist between changes in the Carrier and different stages of sleep.

Long-term measurements collected over many nights are expected to provide the most reliable results.

---

# Current Status

The Dream model remains one of the most experimental parts of the **Measurable Science** project.

Additional long-term measurements are required before reliable conclusions can be drawn.

Every new recording contributes to testing, refining or rejecting the proposed model.

Ultimately, the Dream model will be evaluated only by one criterion:

**Can repeatable relationships between Carrier measurements and sleep be demonstrated under real-world conditions?**
