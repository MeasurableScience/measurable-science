# Submission Guidelines

Thank you for your interest in contributing to the **Measurable Science** project.

These guidelines are intended to maintain the quality, consistency and long-term scientific value of this repository.

---

# Ways to Submit

Measurements may be submitted in one of the following ways.

## Email (Recommended)

Most contributors will find email to be the simplest method.

Please compress your files into a ZIP or 7Z archive before sending.

---

## GitHub Pull Request

Contributors familiar with GitHub are welcome to submit measurements using a Pull Request.

---

# Review Process

All submissions are reviewed before becoming part of this repository.

Submission of data does **not** guarantee publication.

Each submission may be evaluated for:

- completeness of the documentation
- quality of the recorded data
- availability of raw measurements
- consistency with the recommended protocol
- overall usefulness for future research

The project maintainer reserves the right to:

- accept a submission;
- request additional information;
- request corrections;
- request the original raw data;
- decline any submission.

The purpose of this review process is solely to maintain the scientific quality and consistency of the repository.

Submissions are evaluated on documentation quality, completeness and reproducibility — not on whether the results support or contradict any particular hypothesis.

---

# Contributor Responsibilities

By submitting material you confirm that:

- the submitted data are your own work or that you have permission to share them;
- the submitted files have not been intentionally fabricated or falsified;
- the accompanying documentation is accurate to the best of your knowledge;
- you understand that every submission is reviewed before publication.

---

# Licensing

Unless otherwise agreed in writing:

- copyright remains with the original contributor;
- contributors grant the **Measurable Science** project permission to archive, publish and redistribute the submitted material as part of this repository and future project publications;
- all contributors will be credited whenever reasonably possible.

---

# Privacy

Please do not include personal information that you do not wish to make publicly available.

If photographs contain identifiable individuals, please ensure you have permission to publish them.

---

# Repository Standards

Contributors are encouraged to follow the recommendations described in **MeasurementProtocol.md**.

Alternative measurement methods are welcome provided they are adequately documented.

Scientific value depends on documentation and reproducibility rather than the type of equipment used.

---

# Questions

If you are uncertain whether your measurements are suitable for this repository, please contact the project maintainer before submitting them.

Questions are always welcome.
