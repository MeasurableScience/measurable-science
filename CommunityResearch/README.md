# Community Research

Science advances through independent observation, reproducible measurements and open collaboration.

The purpose of this directory is to encourage researchers, engineers, radio amateurs, electronics enthusiasts, students and anyone interested in scientific measurements to contribute independent datasets.

The goal of this project is **not** to prove any particular theory.

The goal is to collect reproducible measurements that can be independently verified, compared and analyzed.

---

# What Is Being Measured?

The current research focuses on measurements containing information within the **0–20 Hz** frequency range.

Your equipment does **not** need to be limited to this range.

You are free to record at higher frequencies and with any sampling rate.

The only requirement is that the recorded data contain usable information within the **0–20 Hz** range.

# Scientific Collaboration

Independent verification is one of the foundations of science.

Positive, negative, unexpected and contradictory results are all valuable when they are properly documented.

Good science is not built by agreement.

It is built by independent observations that anyone can repeat.

**If you can measure, you can contribute.**
